Student readiness checklist

  • MFA is on for my campus email and student portal. [guidance]
  • I use a password manager (or at least a unique password for school accounts). [guidance]
  • My laptop and phone install updates automatically. [guidance]
  • I have at least one backup of my coursework outside the device I use daily. [guidance]
  • I know how to report a phishing email on my campus. [guidance]
  • The IT help desk number is saved in my phone. [guidance]
  • I've reviewed my account recovery email and phone in the last 6 months. [guidance]
